# Sales process

One section per step (call 1, call 2, group call, close, and any others the evidence needs), each with:
- **Goal:** what has to be true by the end of the step.
- **Move on when:** the exit. "More or less" doesn't count.
- **Script:** how the call runs.
- **Materials:** what call prep makes for it (deck, buyer doc, agreement).
- **Follow-up:** what goes out after it.

Each change is a new dated version on top, with what changed and why.

Written by AI Rob during `setup`, then kept current by the weekly review. Empty until then.
